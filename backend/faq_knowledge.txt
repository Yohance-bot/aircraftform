Q: What time does camp run?
A: 9AM to 11AM daily, 2 hours per day.

Q: How many days is the camp?
A: 10 days total.

Q: What age group is this for?
A: Children aged 6 to 14 years, Grades 1 to 10.

Q: Where is it held?
A: Palm Meadows, Whitefield, Bangalore.
   Exclusive for residents.

Q: What will kids learn?
A: Aeromodelling — building RC planes, balsa gliders,
   and drone basics. Hands-on building and flying.

Q: Is food provided?
A: Water bottles and a small snack only. No full meals.
   Please have breakfast before camp.

Q: What should my child bring?
A: Just a water bottle and comfortable clothes.
   Everything else is provided.

Q: How many spots are available?
A: Limited spots per batch. Contact us to confirm
   availability.

Q: What is the price?
A: Please contact us directly for current pricing.
   Payment details are shared after registration.

Q: How do I pay?
A: Payment details are shared within 24 hours
   of registration via WhatsApp and email.

Q: Can siblings join the same batch?
A: Yes! Mention it in the special requirements field
   when registering and we will accommodate.

Q: Is there parking?
A: Yes, Palm Meadows has resident parking available.

Q: What if my child misses a day?
A: Contact our team and we will do our best to
   help catch up. No refunds for missed days.

Q: Is this only for Palm Meadows residents?
A: The current batch is exclusive to Palm Meadows
   residents. Contact us for other arrangements.
